A good agenda answers three questions before the meeting starts: Why are we here? What needs to happen? Who owns each part?

01 • Learn it

The five-part agenda

Strong agendas are short, specific, and designed around outcomes—not just topics.

What makes an agenda useful?

Use this structure every time. You can skip the lesson once it becomes second nature.

1
PurposeWhy does this meeting need to happen?
2
OutcomesWhat must be decided, understood, or assigned?
3
TopicsWhat information, discussion, decisions, or work are needed?
4
Owner + TimeWho leads each item, and how much time does it deserve?
5
CloseConfirm decisions, actions, owners, and what happens next.
